
# NIBBLES

### Narrative Intervention for Behavioural, Learning, Emotional & Social Development

NIBBLES is a **psychology-driven narrative intervention platform** that uses engaging **audio flipbooks and interactive digital experiences** to support behavioural, learning, emotional and social development in children and adolescents.

NIBBLES is designed for **psychologists, parents, caregivers and teachers** to use as a structured intervention and support tool—not simply as digital content.

## What NIBBLES Provides

NIBBLES transforms psychologically informed narratives into **audio flipbooks** that can be used during guided sessions or independently by caregivers and educators.

The interventions can support areas including:

* 🧠 **Behavioural Development** — self-regulation, behaviour patterns, coping and decision-making
* 📚 **Learning** — attention, motivation, learning behaviours and cognitive engagement
* ❤️ **Emotional Development** — emotional awareness, expression, regulation, resilience and coping
* 🤝 **Social Development** — empathy, communication, relationships, perspective-taking and social problem-solving

## Designed For

### 👩‍⚕️ Psychologists

Use NIBBLES as a structured **narrative intervention tool** during supervised psychological sessions, counselling and developmental interventions.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parents & Caregivers

Use age-appropriate audio flipbooks to initiate conversations, encourage emotional expression, reinforce positive behaviour and support everyday development.

### 👩‍🏫 Teachers

Use structured narratives and activities to support social-emotional development, behavioural understanding, classroom wellbeing and learning-related challenges.

## From Story to Intervention

NIBBLES is designed around:

**Psychological Objective → Narrative Intervention → Interaction → Reflection → Behavioural Response → Observation → Follow-up**

The goal is to move beyond simply asking a child to **consume content** and instead create an opportunity for the child to **experience, understand, discuss, practise and apply** psychologically meaningful concepts.

## Audio Flipbooks

NIBBLES audio flipbooks combine **visual narrative, audio narration, interaction and psychologically structured experiences**.

They are designed to be:

* engaging for children
* easy for adults to facilitate
* suitable for guided sessions
* usable by parents and caregivers
* practical for teachers
* adaptable to different developmental levels
* suitable for multilingual intervention environments

## Research-Driven Intervention

NIBBLES is being developed around a continuous research and intervention cycle:

```text
Psychological Research
        ↓
Intervention Design
        ↓
Narrative Development
        ↓
Supervised / Guided Use
        ↓
Observation
        ↓
Response & Outcome Measurement
        ↓
Research & Analysis
        ↓
Intervention Refinement
```

This creates a framework for studying not merely whether a child **uses** an intervention, but whether the intervention produces meaningful behavioural, emotional, learning or social responses.

## Human-Centred Technology

NIBBLES is designed to **support psychologists, parents, caregivers and teachers—not replace them**.

The technology provides structured intervention experiences while human adults remain central to:

* understanding the child
* interpreting responses
* providing emotional support
* making professional judgements
* deciding when additional support is required

## Vision

NIBBLES aims to create a new generation of **technology-assisted psychological intervention** in which psychology, narrative science, research and digital technology work together to make meaningful developmental and emotional support more accessible.
